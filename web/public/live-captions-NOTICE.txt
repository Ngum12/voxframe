Voxframe's live captions (D-196)
================================

The studio draws captions over the video with JASSUB 2.5.16, which is libass
(and the libraries it needs) compiled to WebAssembly. The files are:

  assets/jassub-worker-*.wasm         libass and its libraries, built for the
  assets/jassub-worker-modern-*.wasm  browser (two builds: with and without SIMD)
  assets/jassub-worker-*.js           their loader
  assets/worker-*.js                  JASSUB's worker
  assets/default-*.woff2              JASSUB's fallback font (Liberation Sans)

Their licenses (SPDX: LGPL-2.1-or-later AND (FTL OR GPL-2.0-or-later) AND MIT
AND MIT-Modern-Variant AND ISC AND NTP AND Zlib AND BSL-1.0):

  JASSUB             MIT                  jassub-LICENSE.txt, beside this file
  libass             ISC
  FriBidi            LGPL-2.1-or-later
  FreeType           FTL (chosen of FTL OR GPL-2.0-or-later)
  HarfBuzz           MIT-Modern-Variant
  zlib               Zlib
  Liberation Sans    OFL-1.1              (the fallback font; captions use Inter)
  and parts of the Emscripten runtime under MIT, NTP and BSL-1.0.

LGPL: FriBidi is part of the WebAssembly files, which are separate files
served unmodified as published. You may replace them with your own build of
the same JASSUB version. Its complete source, with every library above, is at
https://github.com/ThaUnknown/jassub (tag 2.5.16, with its submodules).

Left out, deliberately: JASSUB's dependency rvfc-polyfill (GPL-3.0), which no
supported browser needs, and lfa-ponyfill's online font list, which Voxframe
never uses (web/src/vendor).
